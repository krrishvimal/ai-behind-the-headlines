# Source review and verification · release 2.0 · 15 October 2026

35 selected case records, seven editorial themes and eight jurisdictions. The original 20 records were reviewed and 15 were added. This is a curated educational collection, not every AI incident worldwide, a representative sample, or a legal status service. No guarantee of zero errors is made.

## Evidence review

Each record includes source URLs, publication dates, event context, AI/automation role, affected parties, dated outcome, limitations and editorial lessons. The review object records evidence basis, source-section locators, number/scope checks, corrections and remaining uncertainty. The same review is downloadable as dist/data/verification-audit.csv.

The review checks what the cited sources establish. It is not an independent investigation of underlying events or an exhaustive search of every later proceeding. No independent second reviewer has certified the collection.

Sources include regulator and court records, official investigations, developer acknowledgement, research organisation findings, a litigant representative's settlement account and labelled original journalism. They are not all neutral or equally conclusive. These distinctions are visible in the case notes.

## Remaining limits

Outcome dates describe supporting documents, not necessarily current final status. Ascend and Evolv sources describe proposed settlements; final court disposition is not established here. The Hong Kong investigation's later outcome is not established. Replika's later appeal/payment status is not resolved by its selected announcement.

Full FCC PDF retrieval was limited; the fine and separate acquittal are also covered by the reviewed NHPR account. The official PDF link is preserved. The Robodebt report landing page and official judicial explanation were reviewed; the full large PDF exceeded retrieval limits and is not claimed to have been read in full. PIPC labels its English release an unofficial translation.

Source links do not certify every underlying allegation. Severity, victims, penalties and losses are not combined into a ranking or total. Editorial categories can overlap and a cleared allegation remains a controversy record, not a proven harmful incident.
