# Content reuse and provenance review

Selection follow-up updated 16 October 2026 with AI assistance. Unchanged factual checks retain their earlier dates in the audit. This document is an editorial record, not a legal opinion, permissions certificate or exhaustive plagiarism assessment.

## What is included

The current collection contains 30 local case records. This follow-up checks two restored cases and one new case; earlier records retain their dated checks. The site contains short factual accounts, outcome limitations, editorial lessons and source links. The guided checklists are original task-specific educational interpretations. No third-party incident database was bulk imported. No complete source articles, research reports or transcripts are packaged in the website.

Earlier review revisited The Verge, WIRED, NHPR and Amnesty. The records dependent on those sources are now held out under the stricter selection rule. This was not a machine comparison against every sentence of every cited source. Some policies or sources were inaccessible. Factual wording, technical terms, names and legal outcome labels can overlap with source wording; overlap is not automatically infringement, and different wording is not automatically a rights clearance.

## Assets and privacy

The served visual assets are `icon.svg`, created for the project, and `social-preview.png`, rendered by the project’s Pillow script. Charts are generated from this editorial collection. No TED video, speaker photograph, auction artwork, publisher photograph, external chart image or organisation logo is included. Installed fonts are used to render a bitmap; font files are not redistributed.

The case data contains public case identities where material to understanding the record. It does not include victims’ contact information, home addresses, identity documents or named child victims. The Robert Williams record is now held out with other excluded sources. Public criminal-case identities in the current collection are kept with their dated outcomes and uncertainties. No additional private information was added. The guide accepts predefined choices, not personal documents, and does not persist answers or add analytics.

## Transparency and corrections

Visible methodology and footer text disclose AI-assisted research, writing and coding. Dataset metadata and each case’s source section also identify AI assistance. Checks do not imply independent human expert review. Source publishers retain their rights, and citations do not indicate endorsement.

Rights concerns or factual corrections can be raised through the project’s public GitHub issues. Users are warned not to post private or sensitive information there. A concern may lead to corrections, attribution changes or removal after review. No automated response, turnaround time or publisher approval is promised.

Future additions must record source provenance, preserve uncertainty, minimise personal information and avoid copying article bodies or importing assets without a documented reuse basis. Monetisation or new media reuse requires a fresh review; this note does not grant reuse rights.
